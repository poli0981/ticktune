# v2.0.3 live checklist

The `/api/*` rate limit (`docs/16`, v2.0.3). Same two halves as every release
(`docs/14 §5`): the PR's **preview version** before the tag, then production.

Only the Worker and `wrangler.jsonc` changed in behaviour; the client bundle
differs by its version string alone. So the lines below are the budget itself,
that the endpoints still answer inside it, and that nothing static moved.

⚠️ The budget is per IP and per edge location, and a preview shares its
counters with production (same `namespace_id`): after a burst, **wait a full
minute** before the next line, or it reads the burst's 429.

> **Run on #101's preview (version `ced5fe7b`, 2026-10-27)** by the session that
> made the change: About 2.0.3 with a clean console (headless Chromium, beacon
> fulfilled locally); 70 parallel calls → 64 × `400 invalid_id`, then 6 ×
> `429 {"error":"rate_limited"}` with `application/json`, `nosniff`,
> `max-age=0` and no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`; 70 calls to `/` → all 200
> while the budget was spent, and an unknown path → the 404 page; a minute
> later oEmbed 200 with a title and durations 200 with 214000 ms;
> `verify:csp` 6/6. The push made version `ced5fe7b` and no deployment —
> production stayed on v2.0.2's `c11f5b43`.

> **Results after v2.0.3 (2026-10-27).** #101 merged by rebase at 23:39 UTC
> (`e2e` on its second attempt); tag `v2.0.3` on `8fa1653`, tree identical to
> the preview's, verified against `https://github.com/poli0981.gpg` alone
> before the push; `deploy.yml` green, exactly one new deployment
> (`cf543bcd`). Production: About 2.0.3, clean console, `verify:csp` 6/6.
> ⚠️ **The Worker's binding refused nothing on production** — 110, 165 and 300
> paced calls from one IP, all answered — so the 429 line below was met by the
> zone rule the owner created that day, not by `API_LIMIT` (`docs/10 §6`).
