# v2.0.4 live checklist

The YouTube player's own ⏯ (`docs/16`, v2.0.4; `docs/06 §2`). Same two halves
as every release (`docs/14 §5`): the PR's **preview version** before the tag,
then production.

Only the island changed, and only in YouTube mode. The lines below are the
owner's report itself, against the real player — which the E2E suite cannot
reach: its stub never changes state on its own, and YouTube refuses a
Playwright-driven browser.

Desktop browser, Chromium first. Paste these two links, the short one first so
its end arrives inside a minute:

```
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jNQXAC9IVRw
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ
```

## Before anything — the deploy model

- [ ] **Pushing the branch did not deploy it.** `pnpm wrangler deployments
      list` shows no new deployment after the push; `versions list` shows a new
      version.

## Blocking — on the preview version

- [ ] **About reads 2.0.4.**
- [ ] **⏸ in the player stops the run.** YouTube → the two links → a 5-minute
      countdown → Start. Once the video plays, click **on the video** (YouTube's
      own pause). Within a moment: the countdown stops, ON AIR goes dark, the
      transport shows ▶.
- [ ] **▶ in the player starts it again** — the countdown runs, ON AIR lit, ⏸
      on the transport.
- [ ] **A seek is not a pause.** While it plays, click into the player and press
      `l` (or click the scrubber): the countdown never stops and ON AIR stays
      lit. (The transport may show ▶ for the second the video buffers.)
- [ ] **The app's ⏸, then ▶ in the player** → the run resumes with the music.
- [ ] **⏸ in the player, then the app's ▶** → one click resumes both.
- [ ] **A video's end advances.** The 19-second first video plays out → the
      second starts by itself, and the countdown never stopped.
- [ ] **Diagnostics** (⚙) lists one `TT-YT-009` per pause or play made in the
      player — none for the seek, none for the app's own ⏯. Console clean.
- [ ] `TT_CSP_BASE=<Version URL> pnpm verify:csp` — six cases.

## After the signed tag — production

- [ ] **About reads 2.0.4.**
- [ ] **Exactly one new deployment** in `pnpm wrangler deployments list`.
- [ ] **⏸ and ▶ in the player, once more**, on `https://ticktune.net/app/`: the
      countdown stops and starts with the music.
- [ ] `TT_CSP_BASE=https://ticktune.net pnpm verify:csp` — six cases.
